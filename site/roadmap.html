Why iOS comes next

The shared core was deliberately built for cross-platform reuse — one Double Ratchet, one storage schema, one transport layer.

That means the iOS port is largely a matter of building the native Swift shell on top of a core that already works, rather than rewriting the cryptography. It’s a priority for the next stage of development — and the kind of milestone your support helps reach sooner.
