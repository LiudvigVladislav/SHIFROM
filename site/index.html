Design preview

No phone number. No email. No real name.

None of them is required to use SHIFROM.

The relay never sees plaintext.

End-to-end encrypted: a Double Ratchet over libsodium, keys generated on your phone.

Only Maya’s phone can read it.

Sealed-sender envelopes reduce what the relay learns about the sender.

The relay still observes timing, message sizes, authenticated sessions and delivery destinations.

When one route is blocked, another takes over.

Direct WSS, REALITY, Tor and REST fallback reach the network. Offline mesh is planned.

Direct WSS blocked REALITY passes Tor REST fallback

REALITY makes traffic resemble ordinary TLS. It is not an anonymity system and may not bypass every DPI policy; founder-run tests covered specific networks and dates.

Privacy mode

Three levels of exposure. You choose.

Direct-first. The lowest latency, for open networks.

REALITY first. On the wire it resembles ordinary TLS.

Not an anonymity mode. Limits are documented in the threat model.

Tor only. It never silently downgrades: if Tor can’t connect, it fails visibly.

Text only. Tor does not carry calls.
